College Football Schedule

College football runs from late August through the conference championships in early December, followed by bowl season and the College Football Playoff. Weekly windows include Saturday afternoon kickoffs, primetime matchups, and Thursday/Friday night games. Conference games in the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, and ACC drive the playoff picture every fall.

  • Regular season – August through November
  • Conference championships – first weekend in December
  • Bowl games – late December through early January
  • College Football Playoff – national semifinal and final

NCAA Basketball Schedule

College basketball peaks with March Madness, but the season starts in November with non-conference play and continues through conference tournaments. Look for marquee non-conference events early in the year, then rivalry games and ranked matchups through March.

  • Early season tournaments – multi-team events in November
  • Conference play – January through early March
  • Conference tournaments – the week before Selection Sunday
  • NCAA Tournament – 68 teams, single elimination

Streaming Services and Channels

While many games appear on the schedule above, some are also carried by major networks and streaming platforms:

  • FOX / Big Ten Network – Big Ten and selected national windows

  • ABC / CBS / NBC – primetime and conference showcase games

  • SEC Network, ACC Network, Big 12 on ESPN+ – conference-specific coverage
